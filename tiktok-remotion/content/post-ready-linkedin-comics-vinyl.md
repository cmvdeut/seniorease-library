## Post text

As a child, I knew every comic I owned. Every single one. I never bought a duplicate. I knew them all.

As an adult, I built a vinyl collection the same way. For years I could tell you exactly what I had.

Then the collection got too big for my memory.

Standing at a record fair, holding a familiar sleeve: had I bought this already? I stood there for 5 minutes. I bought it. I had it at home.

I tried notebooks. Spreadsheets. Photos of my shelves. None of it was there when I needed it. At the fair, with a record in my hand.

So I built an app.

Scan the ISBN or EAN. See if you own it. That's it.

SeniorEase Library works for books, vinyl, CDs, DVDs and games. No account. No subscription. No cloud. Just your collection, on your phone.

Free to try. €4.99 to unlock everything, once.

Do you still track your collection in your head, or do you use something else? I'd love to hear what works for you.
