# Set up SeniorEase with your parent
## 10 items in 30 minutes — free guide

*Print this or follow at seniorease.eu/family-setup*

---

### Before you start (5 min)

- [ ] Install **SeniorEase Library** from Google Play on your parent's Android phone
- [ ] Open the app — no account needed
- [ ] Sit together with a small stack of books **or** a few records
- [ ] Good light helps the camera scan barcodes

---

### Scan 10 items together (20 min)

For each item:

1. Tap **+** to add
2. Choose **Scan barcode**
3. Point at the ISBN (books) or barcode on the sleeve (vinyl)
4. Confirm title — save
5. Put it back on the shelf

---

### Test before buying (5 min)

- Scan the barcode **before paying** at a shop or market
- Already in the list? → put it back
- Not in the list? → safe to buy

> **Scan first. Buy second.** Works offline.
