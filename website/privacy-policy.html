Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

SeniorEase Library is designed with your privacy in mind. This policy explains how we handle your information.

What We Don't Collect

  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not require an account
  • We do not show ads
  • We do not track your usage

How the App Works

All your book information is stored only on your device. The app works entirely offline after you download it.

When you scan a barcode, the app may connect to public databases (like Google Books) to find book information. This information is only used to fill in details about your books.

Your Data

Everything you add to the app stays on your phone. We never see or access your information. If you delete the app, all your data is removed from your device.

Camera Access

If you choose to scan barcodes, the app will ask for camera permission. The camera is only used for scanning barcodes. We do not store or share any camera images.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy, please contact us.
