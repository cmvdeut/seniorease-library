Book Tracker App Without Account (Android)

By Maureen · July 2026 · 4 min read

I wanted a book tracker. What I did not want was another account.

Every app I tried asked for an email address before I could add a single book. Some wanted me to connect with friends. Others wanted to recommend what I should read next. I just wanted a private list of what I own — so I could stop buying the same paperback twice at the charity shop.

That is why I built SeniorEase Library differently. No account. No sign-up. No cloud. Your books stay on your phone.

Why a book tracker without an account matters

Your book collection is personal. Maybe you have three hundred novels on a shelf. Maybe you collect first editions you would rather not broadcast to the internet. Maybe you simply do not want another password to remember.

Most book apps treat your collection as social content — something to share, rate and discuss. A private book catalog on Android should work the other way around: your data stays local, searchable, and entirely under your control.

SeniorEase Library stores everything on your device. No server. No sync. No one else's algorithm deciding what you should read.

How it works: scan, save, check anywhere

Adding books takes seconds. Hold your phone over the ISBN barcode on the back cover and the app fills in the title, author and cover image. Tap save. Done.

  1. 1
    Download from Google Play. Open the app — no email, no password, no onboarding survey. You land straight in your empty collection.
  2. 2
    Scan your shelf. Pick up a book, tap scan, point the camera at the barcode. Title and author appear automatically. Add it with one tap.
  3. 3
    Check before you buy. At a secondhand shop or library sale, search your collection on the spot. If it is already there, put the book back and save your money.

Frequently asked questions

  • Can I track my books without creating an account?

    Yes. SeniorEase Library stores your entire book catalog locally on your Android phone. There is no sign-up, no email address, and no account to manage. Open the app and start scanning.

  • Is my book collection stored in the cloud?

    No. Your collection stays on your phone only. SeniorEase Library does not upload your books to a server or sync them to the cloud. Your private book catalog is yours alone.
