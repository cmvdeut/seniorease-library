Best App to Track Your Video Game Collection (Android)

By Maureen · June 2026 · 4 min read

Physical game collections have a way of growing faster than you expect. A few titles from a sale here, a second-hand find there, a gift nobody asked for but kept anyway. Before long you're looking at a shelf — or a box — and genuinely unsure what's in it.

And then someone offers you a game at a market and you have to guess.

Why game collections are hard to track

Games come in cases that all look similar. Titles blur together across platforms. You might have the same game on two different consoles and not realize it, or buy something you already own because it came in a different box.

Most collectors try to keep a mental list. Some use a spreadsheet. Both work until the collection gets big enough, and then they don't.

The problem with a spreadsheet is that it's never with you when you need it — at a second-hand shop, at a market, at a friend's house who's selling off their collection.

What you actually need

Something fast. Something that's on your phone. Something that doesn't require an account or a monthly subscription just to know what you own.

You pick up a game, you scan the barcode on the back, and within seconds you know: already in my collection, or not. That's the whole job.
