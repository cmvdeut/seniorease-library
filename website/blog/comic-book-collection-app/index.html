Best App to Track Your Comic Book Collection (Android)

By Maureen · June 2026 · 4 min read

Comic collections are easy to start and surprisingly hard to keep track of. One issue leads to another, a series turns into a run, and before long you have stacks you haven't looked at in years — and no idea which issues you're actually missing.

Or worse: you find an issue at a market and can't remember if you already have it.

The problem with tracking comics

Books you can check off a list. Vinyl you remember buying. But comics come in series, with issue numbers, variant covers, collected editions, and omnibuses — the same story in four different formats. Knowing what you have requires a system.

Most collectors use a notebook or a spreadsheet. Both work until the collection gets big enough that maintaining the list becomes a job in itself. And neither helps you at the moment you need it — standing at a second-hand stall with an issue in your hand.

A faster way to track your comics

Modern comics and graphic novels have barcodes. So do collected editions, hardcovers, and trade paperbacks. That barcode is the fastest way to add something to a list — faster than typing, faster than searching by title.

Scan it, and you're done. The app looks up the title and saves it. At the market stall, you scan first and decide after. No guessing.
