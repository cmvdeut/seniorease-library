Stop Buying Vinyl You Already Own

By Maureen · May 2026 · 4 min read

It happened to me at a record fair. I picked up a Miles Davis record, thought it looked familiar, but couldn't be sure — and bought it anyway. When I got home, there it was on the shelf. The same record. I had two copies now.

If you collect vinyl, you know this feeling. A large collection is a joy. But the moment you're standing at a crate of records, your memory becomes unreliable. Did I already buy this? Is this a different pressing? I should know — but I don't.

The problem with a big vinyl collection

The bigger your collection, the harder it becomes to keep track of what you own. Most collectors rely on memory. A few use a notebook. Some take photos. None of these methods work well when you're standing at a record fair with a stack of interesting records in one hand and a budget in the other.

What you need is a way to instantly check whether a record is already in your collection — from your phone, without Wi-Fi, in 10 seconds flat.

How SeniorEase Library solved it for me

SeniorEase Library is an Android app that lets you scan the barcode on a record, book, DVD or game — and it looks up the title, artist and cover automatically. Everything gets saved in your personal collection on your phone.

No account needed. No cloud. No monthly fee. Just your collection, stored locally on your device.

The first time I used it at a record fair, I scanned every record I was even slightly unsure about. Within seconds I could see whether I already owned it. I stopped buying doubles. I started buying records I actually wanted.

How to use it at the record fair: 3 steps

  1. 1
    Scan your collection at home. Open the app, tap the scan button, and hold your phone over the barcode on each record. The app fills in the title and artist automatically. Spend an afternoon scanning — it's faster than you think.
  2. 2
    Bring your phone to the record fair. Your saved collection is always available offline — no internet needed. Open the app and search by artist or title to check if something is already in your list.
  3. 3
    Scan before you buy. When a record catches your eye, scan its barcode. If it's not in your collection yet, you'll see a "not found" result and can add it. If it is already there, you'll see it instantly — and save yourself €15.

Works for more than vinyl

Most collectors don't stop at records. SeniorEase Library works for your entire collection in one app:

  • Books — scan the ISBN barcode on the back cover
  • CDs and music — same as vinyl, barcode on the case
  • DVDs and Blu-rays — works on all standard disc cases
  • Video games — scan the barcode on the game box

Everything in one place. One search to find whether you already own something, no matter what it is.
