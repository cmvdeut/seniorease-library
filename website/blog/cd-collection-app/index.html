Best App to Track Your CD Collection (Android)

By Maureen · June 2026 · 4 min read

CDs are the collection nobody talks about anymore. They're not as fashionable as vinyl, not as tidy as a digital library. They live in towers, drawers, boxes in the spare room — and because you don't see them every day, you forget what you have.

Until you come home from a charity shop with something you already own.

The problem with a big CD collection

Books you can see on a shelf. Vinyl takes up space you notice. But CDs stack up invisibly — in a cupboard, a car, a box from the last house move. You might have 200. You might have 400. It's surprisingly easy not to know.

And when you spot a familiar title for €1 at a market stall, you face the same question every collector knows: do I already have this one?

With CDs the answer is genuinely hard to know. You can't picture the whole collection in your head the way you might with a small shelf of favourites. So you guess, or you buy it anyway — and sometimes you come home to find you've done it again.

What a CD collection app needs to do

The app needs to be fast. You're standing at a market stall, not at a desk. You don't want to type album names or scroll through a long list. You want to point your phone at a barcode and get an instant answer.

It also needs to work without an account. Your CD collection is personal. You shouldn't need to sign up, sync to a cloud service, or pay monthly to know what's in your own cupboard.
