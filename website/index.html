The story behind the app

Het verhaal achter de app

I'm Maureen, a senior and book lover. I love browsing second-hand book markets — but I kept coming home with books I already owned.

You pick up an interesting title, it's only €1, you buy it... and at home it turns out you already have it on your shelf. Sound familiar?

I looked for a simple app to track my library. Everything I found was too complicated, needed an account, or had a subscription I didn't want. So I learned to code and built SeniorEase Library myself.

Scan the ISBN on the back of a book — it's added to your library instantly. Next time you're at a book market, just check your phone.
