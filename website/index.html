How to Install

  1. Click the download button above or scan the QR code
  2. When asked to "Install unknown apps", tap "Allow from this source"
  3. If you see a "Google Play Protect" warning, tap "Scan app" or "Install anyway" - this is normal for apps not from the Play Store
  4. Tap "Install" and wait for it to complete
  5. Open the app and start scanning your books!
