Welcome to SeniorEase Library

A simple and easy-to-use app to keep track of your books, music, DVDs, and games. Scan barcodes with your phone camera to add items to your collection instantly.

Demo Version: This free demo version lets you scan and save up to 10 items. Perfect for trying out the app before upgrading to the full version with unlimited items.

📥 How to Install:

  1. Click the download button above or scan the QR code
  2. Open the downloaded file on your Android phone or tablet
  3. If you see a message about "Unknown sources":
    • Go to: Settings → Security → Allow unknown sources
    • Or: Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps
  4. Tap "Install" and wait for the installation to complete
  5. Open the app and start scanning your collection!
